SILK HOUSE

A desktop application for producing and publishing YouTube videos, run by its author on their own computer.

SILK HOUSE takes a topic through research, scripting, review, narration, storyboarding, footage selection and rendering, and then uploads the finished video privately to a YouTube channel its operator owns. Every stage is reviewable, and nothing is published without an explicit approval.

What it connects to

Only what the operator configures, and only on their own machine:

  • YouTube — uploading videos to channels the operator owns, and reading those channels' own analytics.
  • An OpenAI-compatible endpoint — writing and reviewing scripts.
  • Pexels and Pixabay — searching for stock footage and photographs.
  • RunPod — optional image generation on rented GPUs.

Narration is synthesised locally; no audio leaves the machine. There is no server, no account to create and no shared installation: the application runs entirely on the operator's computer and stores its work there.

Who it is for

This is a private tool, built and used by one person for their own YouTube channels. It is not distributed, not sold and not offered as a service.
